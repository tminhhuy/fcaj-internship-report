Contract review takes expert time, and general-purpose AI is not yet reliable enough to be used on its own. Research has measured legal hallucination rates of 58–88% for general chatbots and 17–33% for legal tools that already use document retrieval.

This part explains the problem SCA solves, the research each design decision is based on, and the seven principles that shape every later choice.

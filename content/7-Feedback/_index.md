### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The environment is friendly and open. Mentors and FCAJ peers are always ready to help, and the workspace is comfortable and easy to focus in.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
Mentors give detailed guidance and encourage me to investigate and solve problems on my own first, which helps me retain knowledge. The admin team handles procedures and documents well.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The internship builds directly on the networking, operating systems and programming I learned at university, and extends it into cloud computing.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
I practiced hands-on with AWS, learned to read technical documentation, worked in a team and presented architecture to mentors.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
People respect each other, work seriously while keeping a positive atmosphere, and readily share experience.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
The program offers flexible time and many useful sharing sessions and technical events.

### Additional Questions

- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?
   * Hands-on practice on AWS and direct mentor feedback on the project architecture.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?
   * A suggested week-by-week roadmap would help interns track progress.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?
   * Yes, FCAJ builds a solid cloud foundation and gives clear career direction.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?
   * More cross-team architecture review sessions to learn from each other.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?
   * Yes, I want to keep pursuing cloud and AWS certifications.
